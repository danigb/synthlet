---
title: Pitch, loudness and timbre
description: Three words for three properties of a sound, and the three knobs that change them one at a time.
---

Pitch is how fast the wave repeats. Loudness is how far it swings. Timbre is
everything else — the shape of the swing, which is to say which harmonics are
there and how loud each of them is.

They are worth separating early because synthesiser controls rarely respect the
separation: turn up a filter and the sound gets brighter _and_ louder, so it is
easy to learn the wrong lesson about what a knob did.

This lesson is a placeholder. One oscillator and three controls, each moving
exactly one of the three, arrive with the first chapter's real content.
