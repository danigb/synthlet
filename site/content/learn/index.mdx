Most people meet a synthesiser as a wall of knobs and a preset list. This is the
other way round: one idea at a time, one live patch, one or two knobs, and at the
bottom of every lesson the handful of lines that made the noise you just heard.

You need no hardware, no theory and no account. You need a browser that can play
audio and a pair of headphones, because a lot of what is worth hearing here does
not survive a laptop speaker.

## How to read it

Read in order if you are starting out. The chapters build: sound before the
things that shape it, shaping before the things that move the shapers. The
lessons marked **core** are the short path through — the twenty that get you
from a first note to a sound of your own, and nothing else.

Skip around if you already know the words. Every lesson stands on its own patch,
and the patch is always visible.

The arrow keys turn the page. Lessons you have opened are ticked, in this
browser only: there is no account here and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Where it comes from

This tutorial is written in the open and stands on other people's work. The
