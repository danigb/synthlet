This is a synthesiser. It has thirty-one knobs and you cannot see one of them,
which is on purpose: you are going to take this instrument apart a knob at a
time for the next ten chapters, and it is worth hearing it whole first.

<Patch id="voice" preset="Init" show={["keyboard"]} />

<Try>

Press **Play**, then press a key — with the pointer, or with the letter keys on
your computer keyboard. Hold one down and let go, and listen to the two ends of
the note: how it arrives, and how long it takes to leave. Then press three keys
at once.

</Try>

Nothing in what you just heard is complicated. One part of it makes a raw,
buzzy tone. Another takes the top off that tone, which is what decides whether
it sounds bright or dull. A third decides how loud the note is at each instant,
which is why it faded instead of stopping dead. The chapters after this one are
those three parts, in that order, one idea and one or two knobs at a time.

The three keys at once are worth a moment. Each key you press takes a whole
copy of that chain for itself, and there are eight copies waiting. That is all
the word <Term>polyphonic</Term> means.

## The code is the lesson

Under every widget here there is a line that says **View the code**. Open it.
What you get is not a sketch of the patch: it is the file running in your
browser at this moment, imports and `connect()` calls and all, and you can paste
it into a page of your own. That is the one thing this tutorial has that the
two it is built on do not — [where the ideas come from](/learn/about) says whose
they are.

At the foot of this page is a link to the Playground: the same synth with every
knob showing. It will make no sense yet. It will by chapter 5.
